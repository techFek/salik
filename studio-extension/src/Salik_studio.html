Sync to platform cut

If the video you're censoring runs on different timing than this subtitle (different release, extra intro, trimmed scenes), add 2+ reference points and re-time everything. Only Language rows are shifted — your manually-placed Nudity/Violence/Substance abuse cuts are left untouched.

Exact word timing (audio)

Listens to the real audio of each line that has a muted word (Whisper, in your browser) and moves the mute onto the word itself. Needs the film's sound as a WAV file from the same release as the .srt. Make it once with FFmpeg:

ffmpeg -i film.mkv -vn -ac 1 -ar 16000 film.wav

Detection lists

Single-word profanity

One word per line. Matched exactly against each spoken word.

Multi-word phrases (mute)

One phrase per line, e.g. "sleep with". Matched against the whole line, timed across the matched words.

Nudity-content patterns

One regex per line (case-insensitive). Flags the full line for manual review — never auto-muted.
